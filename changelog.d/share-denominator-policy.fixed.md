- A share's denominator is held to the caller's policies as the total over the same columns
  is: under a policy that requires a grouping, a share over that grouping is refused with
  `POLICY_DENIED`, naming the grouping and the share, where it used to return the total the
  policy refuses (the value divided by its share). A share within the required grouping, over
  the period or another column, still answers. See
  [Share of a total](docs/QUERY_CALL.md#share-of-a-total).
