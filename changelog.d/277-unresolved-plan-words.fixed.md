- Plans with an unknown question word left unresolved and unconsumed no longer offer execute
  readiness, including a city whose filter value has no declared domain. The recovery hint
  asks for an explicit filter or a revised question.

  second grain cannot leave a plan ready. Count measures used only in filters no longer
