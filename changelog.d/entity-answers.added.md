- Graph entities accept `display:`, the dimension that names one row in an answer. `plan`
  answers "which accounts closed last week", "who upgraded last week", "top 2 accounts by MRR"
  and "each plan" with the entity's rows: its key and display dimension, ranked with the stated
  count and direction ("top three", "bottom 1") or listed by name without zero rows. "Who" that
  could mean several entities asks which.
